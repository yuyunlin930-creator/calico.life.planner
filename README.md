Calico Life Planner is a single-page web app that brings long-term life goals and short-term plans into one place. Set goals with tasks, numeric targets or manual progress, log study time with a built-in stopwatch, and watch your daily effort on a heatmap. Finishing tasks earns coins to feed your calico cat. Neglect it for too long, and it runs away. Everything lives in one index.html and is saved in your browser’s localStorage. English and Traditional Chinese, light and dark themes.
